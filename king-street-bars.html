Top 5 King Street Bars for Bachelorette Parties

Your ultimate guide to the best nightlife spots in Downtown Charleston

Planning a bachelorette party in Charleston? King Street is your one-stop destination for an epic night out. From rooftop cocktails to late-night dancing, this iconic stretch has everything you need to celebrate in style. We've rounded up the top five bars that bachelorette groups absolutely love.

1

Prohibition

Rooftop Bar Dancing Late Night

547 King Street

Prohibition is the crown jewel of King Street nightlife. This multi-level venue features a stunning rooftop bar with panoramic views of Charleston, making it perfect for those Instagram-worthy bachelorette moments. The vibe transitions from sophisticated cocktail lounge early evening to high-energy dance club as the night progresses.

Why bachelorettes love it: The rooftop atmosphere is unbeatable for group photos, the DJ keeps the energy high, and the central location makes it easy to bar hop before or after. Request a reserved table if you have a larger group.

Best time to visit: 10 PM - 2 AM for dancing | 8 PM for rooftop cocktails

2

Republic Garden & Lounge

Multi-Level Live Music Dance Floor

489 King Street

Republic is a bachelorette party staple for good reason. This three-story venue offers different vibes on each level—from a garden patio perfect for early drinks to an upstairs dance floor where things get wild. The eclectic decor and laid-back atmosphere make it easy for groups to settle in and have a blast.

Why bachelorettes love it: The variety means everyone in your group will find their happy place. Start downstairs with drinks, move to the garden for fresh air, then head upstairs when you're ready to dance. The friendly staff is used to handling bachelorette crowds.

Best time to visit: 9 PM onwards | Check their event calendar for live music nights

3

The Commodore

Craft Cocktails Dancing Upscale

337 King Street

The Commodore brings sophistication to the King Street bar scene. This nautical-themed cocktail lounge serves up creative drinks in a stylish setting. While it starts as a classy cocktail spot, the energy ramps up later with DJs spinning dance-worthy tracks.

Why bachelorettes love it: The craft cocktails are phenomenal (try their signature drinks), the decor is chic and photo-friendly, and it's a great spot to start your night before hitting the rowdier venues. The bartenders are true mixology artists.

Best time to visit: 7-9 PM for cocktails | 10 PM+ for dancing

4

Trio

Dive Bar Vibe Casual Local Favorite

177 Meeting Street (near King)

Don't let the dive bar aesthetic fool you—Trio is legendary among locals and bachelorette groups alike. This no-frills spot serves strong, affordable drinks in a fun, unpretentious atmosphere. It's the perfect place to let loose without worrying about being too polished.

Why bachelorettes love it: The drinks are cheap, the crowd is fun, and there's zero pretension. It's a great palate cleanser between fancier venues, and the bartenders pour generously. Cash only, so hit the ATM first!

Best time to visit: Anytime! Great for starting early or as a mid-crawl stop

5

Myrtles

Sports Bar Games Laid Back

311 King Street

Myrtles brings a fun, casual sports bar energy to Upper King. With games, TVs, and a relaxed atmosphere, it's perfect for groups who want to kick back with drinks without the pressure of a nightclub scene. The outdoor seating area is a bonus on nice evenings.

Why bachelorettes love it: Sometimes you just want good drinks, bar games, and easy conversation. Myrtles delivers exactly that. It's especially great for daytime or early evening stops before hitting the dance clubs. The patio is perfect for group hangs.

Best time to visit: 5-8 PM for happy hour vibes | Anytime for a casual atmosphere

Pro Tips for Your King Street Bar Crawl

  • Start early: Bars get packed after 10 PM on weekends. Arrive around 8-9 PM to snag good spots.
  • Dress code: Most venues enforce a "no hats, no athletic wear" policy. Dress to impress!
  • Transportation: King Street is walkable, but Uber/Lyft are your friends for getting to and from your hotel or Airbnb.
  • Cover charges: Some venues have cover charges after 10 PM on weekends ($5-15 typically).
  • Group size: If you have 8+ people, consider calling ahead for table reservations at Republic and Prohibition.
