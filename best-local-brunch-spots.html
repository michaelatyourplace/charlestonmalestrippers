Waterfront Vibes

Red Drum

803 Coleman Blvd, Mount Pleasant, SC 29464

Why You Need This After Last Night:

Nothing cures a hangover quite like waterfront views and fresh seafood. Red Drum sits on Shem Creek, offering both indoor and outdoor seating with gorgeous water views. Must Order for Recovery: The She-Crab Soup followed by their Crab Cake Benedict. The soup is rich and warming, perfect for settling queasy stomachs, while the Benedict provides protein and those glorious hollandaise-covered carbs you're craving.

Pro Tips for Brunch Recovery

  • Timing is Everything: Most Mt. Pleasant brunch spots get packed between 10am-12pm on weekends. If possible, aim for 9am or after 1pm to avoid long waits when you're already feeling rough.
  • Hydrate Before You Arrive: Chug water before you leave for brunch. You'll feel 50% better before you even order food.
  • Order Extra Sides: When hungover, your group will eat more than usual. Order extra bacon, hash browns, or biscuits to share.
  • Embrace the Grease: This is not the time for egg white omelets. Your body wants comfort food—give it what it needs.
  • Split a Dessert: Many places have amazing beignets or cinnamon rolls. The sugar rush helps more than you'd think.
  • Take Your Time: Don't rush the recovery meal. Linger, chat about last night's highlights, and let your body slowly come back to life.

What to Look for in a Post-Party Brunch Spot

Generous Portions

After a night of celebration, your body is depleted and craving substantial food. The best recovery brunch spots don't skimp on portion sizes. You want plates that arrive loaded with eggs, meat, potatoes, and carbs. Mount Pleasant restaurants understand Southern hospitality, which means you won't leave hungry.

Carb-Heavy Options

Science backs this up: your body processes alcohol using glycogen stored in your liver, and replenishing those stores requires carbohydrates. French toast, biscuits, grits, hash browns, and pancakes aren't just delicious—they're actually helping your recovery. Don't fight the craving for bread and potatoes; lean into it.

Hydration-Friendly Menus

Look for places that offer fresh-squeezed juices, coconut water, or electrolyte-rich beverages alongside their coffee. While that mimosa might sound tempting (hair of the dog and all), your body actually needs serious hydration first. Coffee is great, but make sure you're also drinking water or juice.

Casual, Judgment-Free Atmosphere

You and your crew probably aren't looking your best this morning. The ideal post-party brunch spot has a relaxed, come-as-you-are vibe where showing up in athletic wear and sunglasses is perfectly acceptable. Mount Pleasant excels at this kind of laid-back coastal dining.

Recovery Hack: The Charleston Breakfast Sandwich Cure

Almost every spot on this list offers some version of a breakfast sandwich. There's something about the combination of bread, egg, cheese, and meat that specifically targets hangover symptoms. The protein stabilizes blood sugar, the carbs restore energy, and the fat helps your body process any lingering alcohol. Order one—your future self will thank you.

Planning Your Post-Party Brunch

Location Matters

If you celebrated in downtown Charleston and are staying in Mount Pleasant, you're perfectly positioned for these brunch spots. Most are within 10-15 minutes of each other, so you can choose based on vibe rather than distance. Shem Creek restaurants (Red Drum, Water's Edge) are great if you want waterfront views. The Highway 17 spots (Toast!, Shelter) are convenient if you're coming from farther north in Mount Pleasant.

Make Reservations When Possible

While some of these spots don't take reservations, others do—and when you're hungover, the last thing you want is a 45-minute wait. Call ahead or check their websites. If reservations aren't available, send one person from your group ahead to put your name on the waitlist while everyone else gets ready.

Consider the Vibe Your Group Needs

Every group has different recovery needs. Some want quiet, peaceful settings to nurse their headaches (Water's Edge, Red Drum). Others want lively atmospheres that distract from the pain (Page's Okra Grill, Toast!). Some groups need upscale quality to feel human again (The Obstinate Daughter, Shelter). Think about what kind of energy your crew has and choose accordingly.
